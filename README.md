I'm a third-year at UC Berkeley studying data science and economics. Most of what I build is AI tooling for people who work with documents and property data.

This past summer at Empire State Realty Trust I built Lease Intelligence, a proof-of-concept RAG system over 880 commercial leases. It answers plain-English questions with cited excerpts from the source documents, and tracks upcoming critical dates: rent steps, lease and letter-of-credit expirations, holdovers. Underneath it sit the Harvey agents and playbooks I wrote to read a full lease package and emit abstracts in two formats, one RAG-optimized and one verbatim tabular for Yardi Voyager entry.

Before that, the React frontend and STT/LLM/TTS architecture for a real-time voice agent at Skyline (Berkeley SkyDeck), and an MVP candidate insights platform at CGP in Shanghai. Right now I'm building [CompLens](https://complens-ai.vercel.app/), which turns a property address into a ranked rent comp memo for nearby multifamily communities.

Most of it starts as a spreadsheet someone is tired of maintaining.
